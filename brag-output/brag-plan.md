# Brag Plan: PhotoTrace

## What is this app?
PhotoTrace matches JPEG capture times to a GPX track, previews the locations, and makes geotagged copies while leaving originals untouched.

## The angle
A route remembers where the camera was. Show the practical transformation from a GPX line and loose photos to matched markers, then to a saved outing and copied files. All on-screen names, times, and locations are fictional demo data.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
A camera marker lands on an animated route. Headline: "Your photos have a place." The line settles and holds.

## Key moments
- The actual PhotoTrace flow labels: "Guided", "Preview matches", "Map Preview", and "Create geotagged copies".
- Three photo markers become synchronized with the route and gallery.
- The output ends with separate geotagged copies and originals explicitly untouched.

## Outro
PhotoTrace mark and line: "Follow the route. Keep the originals."

## User flow worth showing
Choose a GPX track and photo folder -> preview matched locations on a map/gallery -> create geotagged copies in a separate folder. Saved outings appear as an optional result.

## Nine-question rubric
1. Desktop application that geotags JPEG copies by matching EXIF times to GPX data.
2. "Source photos are never modified" is the most meaningful product promise.
3. Green route line and matched photo pins across a map/gallery.
4. Recreated Guided workspace and Map Preview, using labels from the current source.
5. 20 seconds: enough to see input, match, and output.
6. Preset: polished. Direction: quiet field-tool film with crisp route motion.
7. Soft local synth pulse and a few restrained local click tones.
8. "PhotoTrace puts trip photos back on the map, then makes geotagged copies while the originals stay untouched."
9. GPX and JPEG input -> map preview -> geotagged copies.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet field-tool film
- Interpretation: restrained type, deliberate route motion, generous holds, no inflated claims.

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents.
- Music: original simple synth bed generated locally with FFmpeg.
- Music treatment: quiet throughout, gentle final fade.
- Music cue guidance: self-generated 100 BPM pulse; 0.6s grid, with key reveals near 4.8s, 10.8s, and 16.8s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; the steady route pulse follows the authored timeline.
- SFX posture: sparse local sine clicks for input, preview, and output.
- Restraint rule: audio must never obscure the information.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Location returns — 4.5s
A route draws, a camera pin lands, and "Your photos have a place." appears. Hold the complete line for more than two seconds.
Sequential/interaction: route draws, then one marker lands.
Audio intent: calm invitation; one soft landing.
Transition mood: clean wipe to Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Inputs — 5.0s
Recreated PhotoTrace Guided workspace: GPX track, JPEG photo folder, timezone, and "Preview matches". Demo file names are fictional. A cursor-like dot activates preview.
Sequential/interaction: input rows settle one by one, then the preview action.
Audio intent: two light clicks.
Transition mood: clean wipe to Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Match review — 5.5s
Map Preview with route, three numbered markers, synchronized gallery thumbnails, and a matched photo detail. Text: "See every match before export."
Sequential/interaction: markers and gallery items appear in matched order, then one pair highlights.
Audio intent: quiet confirmation.
Transition mood: clean wipe to Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Copies and brand — 5.0s
"Create geotagged copies" action yields three output JPEG cards beside a small "Originals untouched" statement. End on PhotoTrace icon and "Follow the route. Keep the originals."
Sequential/interaction: output files appear in order.
Audio intent: one soft completion tone, then fade.
Transition mood: final hold.
